Privacy

Patternaut collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

The app makes no network requests. It has never needed to, so it does not ask for the permission and would have nowhere to send anything if it did.

Your work stays where you put it. Documents are ordinary files on your Mac, and exported projects are written to the folder or SD card you choose. Patternaut reads a folder only when you point it at one, and it writes only where you say.

If you buy the app from the App Store, Apple handles the purchase and tells me nothing about you beyond the anonymous sales figures every developer sees. Apple's own privacy terms cover that part.

There is no data to request, correct or delete, because none is held.

Questions are welcome at hello@iamjarl.com.
