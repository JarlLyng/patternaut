A tracker-native toolkit

Patternaut thinks the way the Tracker works — 16 tracks, per-step FX, monophonic voices — so what you make actually fits the machine.

Generate

Rhythm generators

Euclidean rhythms, probability, swing and humanize — every result reproducible from a seed.

Evolve

Mutation

Nudge a pattern from subtle to chaotic. Keep its identity, or deliberately break it.

Shape

Transforms

Rotate, reverse, and groove your patterns non-destructively across tracks and steps.

Hardware

SD-card export

Write byte-exact .mtp patterns, .mt projects and .pti instruments straight to your card.

Play

Live MIDI

Send a pattern straight into the Tracker in record mode over USB or MIDI.

Native

Local & offline

A real macOS app. No account, no cloud, your work stays on your Mac.

How it works

From idea to hardware in three moves.

Generate or edit

Start from a generator or program the grid by keyboard.

Mutate & shape

Explore variations, compare versions, keep the ones you like.

Take it to the Tracker

Export a project to your SD card, or play it in live over MIDI.
